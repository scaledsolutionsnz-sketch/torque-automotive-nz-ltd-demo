About Torque

A Frankton workshop that treats your vehicle like its own.

Torque Automotive NZ has been the go-to workshop in Frankton for over a decade — a proper local team looking after everyday drivers, tradies, tourism operators and travellers passing through the district.

We keep it straight: tell you what your vehicle actually needs, quote it before we touch it, and do the job to a standard that lasts. No upselling, no jargon — just reliable work from people who know the roads you drive.

  • Locally owned and operated in Queenstown
  • Clear quotes before any work begins
  • Every make and model — cars, 4WDs, vans and campers
