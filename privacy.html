Privacy Policy

How Torque Automotive NZ Ltd handles the information you share with us.

This is placeholder content for demonstration purposes and is not legal advice. A final privacy policy would be tailored to Torque Automotive NZ Ltd before going live.

1. Overview

Torque Automotive NZ Ltd respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it and how we look after it, in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, phone number and email address, when you book a service or enquire.
  • Vehicle information needed to carry out the work, such as make, model, registration and service history.
  • Basic, non-identifying website usage information if you browse this site.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, book and carry out work, contact you about your vehicle, provide quotes and invoices, and keep records required for servicing and compliance.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it where necessary to complete your work, for example with parts suppliers, or where we are required to by law.

5. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and to retain it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

6. Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you and request corrections if it is wrong. Contact us using the details below to make a request.

7. Third-party links

This website may link to external services such as Google Maps and social media. Those services have their own privacy policies, which we recommend you review.

8. Contact us

For any privacy questions, contact Torque Automotive NZ Ltd at 218B Glenda Drive, Frankton, Queenstown 9300, phone 03 441 4421, or email service@torqueautomotive.co.nz.
